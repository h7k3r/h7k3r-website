What it's for

Keyless car theft

Thieves use a relay box to carry your fob's signal from the hallway to the car on the drive. A fob in a closed pouch has nothing to relay.

Tracking

A phone in the pouch can't report its location or be pinged until you take it out.

Meetings and travel

Take a phone fully off every network without switching it off.

Tell us your phone or key fob when you order and we'll confirm it fits.

Good to know

  • Put your phone in, close the top and call it from another phone. It should go straight to voicemail.
  • For a car key: stand next to the car with the fob in the closed pouch and pull the door handle. The car should stay locked.
  • Repeat the test every few months. Folds and seams wear with daily use.
